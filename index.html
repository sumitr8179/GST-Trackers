Import cases from Excel / CSV

Use a case list downloaded from the BO portal or your own register. The first row must be the column headings. You will match the columns before anything is imported. Excel files need an internet connection the first time (to load the reader); CSV always works offline.

Export, backup & sync

Keeping phone and PC in step: take a Backup on one device, open it with "Restore / sync" on the other. Cases are merged — the most recently edited copy of each case wins, and deletions carry across — so nothing is duplicated. Do the same in the other direction to bring both up to date.

Security

With a PIN set, all saved cases are encrypted on this device (AES-256) and the app locks itself after the idle time chosen. If you forget the PIN the data cannot be recovered — keep a backup.
